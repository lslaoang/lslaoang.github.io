Privacy Policy

Under the Hood uploader · Last updated November 24, 2026

"Under the Hood uploader" is a private tool used by the operator of the Under the Hood Facebook Page to upload short educational videos to that Page. It is not offered to the public and does not let other people log in.

What the app accesses

  • The ID and access token of the Under the Hood Page, granted by the Page's own administrator.
  • The list of Pages that administrator manages, only to select the Under the Hood Page.
  • Basic status of videos the app uploads (for example, whether processing finished).

The app does not read, collect, or store personal information about anyone who views, follows, likes, or comments on the Page.

How the information is used and stored

The Page token is stored only on the operator's own computer and is used solely to upload videos and their captions to the Under the Hood Page. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

The app keeps no data about Facebook users. The Page administrator can revoke the app's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations, which immediately stops all access. To request deletion of anything related to this app, email whatsunderthehood@proton.me; requests are handled within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
